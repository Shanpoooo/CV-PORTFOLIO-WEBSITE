# My Portfolio ● The Art of (Computer) Science

## [MAIN PAGE + LAYOUT]

[Open it here.](https://shanpoooo.github.io/CV_2026/)

The attention span of a viewer is ever decreasing, therefore it is taken upon the front-end developers to seize each reader with an accessible, intriguing page. The option to release this website with regular, corporate CSS was available, but what's the joy in that? This Portfolio aims to both demonstrate my accomplishments in Computer Science, as well as allow me to have fun with the graphic design. The main inspirations behind the theme are:

Topics such as my Academic upbringing are boxed in by Windows-XP-esque rectangles. This is done to immerse the reader in the abyssal, surreal world of the design. It even contains a fish on the right side of the window, where the "[X]" button would be; I recommend clicking him with caution.

It is embedded with an optional music player, with basic controls and a few tracks to demonstrate the prowess in musical skill. The names are contained in what might seem like jumbled numbers, but their naming is useful in their development; in "TRACK_2v1D: ASH.MP3", the "TRACK_2" refers to its Index in the JS script, the "v1" refers to the current version hosted by the website (often the final version), "D" refers to its tag (D for my darker OSTS, L for the more upbeat, 'Light" ones) and the MP3 to its file format.

The CSS is both extensive and not flashy, the animations were kept moderate as computers back then weren't capable of fancy keyframes for every action. However the stylization was still prioritized in favor of an immersive experience. The frames, linebreaks, lists and icons alike were done specifically for this project.

# Inspirations

## [Serial Experiments Lain (PS1)]

<img height="340" alt="Game_Screenshot" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/668cf00f-d2f8-42d8-a469-b060df89e505" align="left"/>

This game presents itself in a cryptic, OS-oriented form, serving both in the concept and color-palette of this CV. It requires you to access different chapters, diaries and conversations as though you are wandering inside a digital folder. Serial Experiments Lain was highly impactful both in its forecasting of the modern internet (given its release in 1998) as well as its raw, suffocating yet fascinating experience. This Anime—alongside other existential pieces such as Neon Genesis Evangelion and Perfect Blue—was partly influential in its retro presentation.

## [Frutiger Aero]

<img height="340" alt="Frutiger_Icon" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8218567b-ceb6-42fe-b633-bbf6cfa8021c" align="left"/>

Frutiger Aero—and its siblings—is an aesthetic that thrives in surrealism, colorful nature and nostalgia surrounding the early 2000s. For this website, however, the palette was tweaked, and instead flanked from a visibly darker, although still hopeful palette. It features the same natural concepts and gradients through colors resembling royalty, such as navy blue and mulberry colors. The word "Aero" comes from latin, meaning "Air." Therefore, "Regalia" stands for "Royalty."
